✈️ First Time In Malaysia?

Airport

Fly into Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA).

Transport

We recommend using Grab for transportation.

Currency

The local currency is the Malaysian Ringgit (MYR).

Language

English is widely spoken in Kuala Lumpur.

Weather

Expect warm and humid weather in August.
